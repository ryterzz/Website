gaming

what i play

gaming is probably where most of my free time goes. i play a bunch of different stuff depending on what i'm feeling.

10 games

Minecraft

i play a lot of PvP, mostly on modern versions. i'll still hop on 1.8 sometimes though. i've been playing minecraft for years and PvP is easily one of my favorite parts of the game.

Roblox

i play different games on roblox depending on what looks fun. i'll usually bounce between a few games instead of sticking to one.

Osu!

i think the amount of hours is kinda embarrassing, but i play osu! a lot. i play a lot of different maps, but i mainly play standard. i'd consider myself a good player, even to the point of being semi-professional. i've been playing for a long time and have a lot of experience with the game.

other games

i'll play pretty much anything that looks interesting. FPS games, anime games, random stuff with friends, whatever.

Counter-Strike 2

fps

cs2 is one of those games where every round can get intense. i like the mix of aim, quick decisions, and trying to outplay the other team.

Team Fortress 2

fps

tf2 is just fun to jump into. there are a bunch of different classes, so i can switch up how i play instead of doing the same thing every round.

ULTRAKILL

fps

ultrakill is super fast and over the top. there's a lot going on at once, and getting better at moving around and chaining attacks is really satisfying.

Undertale

indie rpg

undertale has a lot of personality and characters that stick with you. i like that you can choose how you deal with encounters instead of only fighting.

Deltarune

indie rpg

deltarune has the same kind of weird humor and memorable characters i like, with a bigger adventure and a fun battle system.

Terraria

adventure

terraria is great when i want something more relaxed to explore. there's always another item to find, place to build, or boss to try.
